# Scorecard Requirements Summary (from HTML Performance Scorecard)

## Gate Zero: The Handoff Test (Pass/Fail)
Can CEO answer 4 questions in <5 min without opening a spreadsheet?
- Are we good?
- Did we hit target?
- Does revenue cover team?
- Are we on margin?
If FAIL → overall rating capped at "Partially Met"

## Category 1: Financial Machine (Y1: 40%, Y2+: 35%) — Outcome-Owned

### 1A. Reporting Rhythm
- Daily cash email (automated, by 9 AM)
- Weekly ops snapshot (Monday, all 6 metrics)
- Monthly close (rolling-12 P&L by 5th calendar day)
- Quarterly reforecast (first 2 weeks of quarter)

### 1B. Cash and Collections
- 13-week cash forecast (2-week refresh weekly; full 13 at close)
- Invoicing completeness (within 3 biz days of trigger)
- AR aging cadence (every overdue invoice actioned per brackets)
- Tax reserve (set aside quarterly per accountant)

### 1C. Cost Governance
- Salary cap tracking (flagged before any hiring discussion)
- Software & vendor spend (monthly variance; quarterly audit; renewal calendar)
- Budget setup at SOW (live in PM tool within 3 biz days of signature)

### 1D. Systems and Access
- Systems provisioning (Day-1 access; 24h revocation at exit)
- Payroll (on time, every cycle, zero errors)
- AP management (paid on terms, no late fees)

## Category 2: Visibility Engine (Y1: 25%, Y2+: 30%) — Visibility-Owned
Graded on: right (accurate), current (not stale), early (problems surfaced before they hit)

### 2A. Profit and Revenue Visibility
- Pretax profit % vs commitment
- Sales target attainment
- Revenue forecast accuracy

### 2B. Operational Health Visibility
- LER trend
- Billable utilization (blended + per-level)
- Time tracking compliance
- Project burn variance (75% flag within 24h; 90% to CEO)
- Milestone adherence

### 2C. Strategic Risk Visibility
- Client concentration
- Per-client profitability (quarterly ranking)
- Backlog coverage (weeks vs break-even)

## Category 3: Escalation Discipline (Y1: 15%, Y2+: 20%)
Triggers:
- Core capital < 2 months → CEO same day
- Cash shortfall inside 13 weeks → CEO same day
- LER below threshold → monthly close
- Profit below floor for quarter → monthly close
- Project burn 75%/90% → Delivery 24h / CEO
- Salary cap breach → before the offer
- Client concentration threshold → monthly close
- Invoice > 60 days → per aging brackets

## Category 4: Operational Maturity (Y1: 20%, Y2+: 15%)
- Report automation
- Process documentation
- Data quality
- Key-person redundancy (vacation test)
- Operational leverage (revenue growing without proportional ops headcount)

## Rhythms Required
| Cadence | Deliverables |
|---------|-------------|
| Daily | Cash email (automated, 9 AM) |
| Weekly | Ops snapshot (6 metrics), AR aging review, project burn check |
| Monthly | P&L close by 5th, 13-week cash forecast refresh, expense review, client profitability |
| Quarterly | Reforecast with scenario analysis, software audit, client concentration review, tax reserve |

## Protected Floor (Never Stops, Never Degrades)
1. Daily cash email
2. Weekly ops snapshot (6 metrics)
3. Monthly close by the 5th
4. Escalation triggers
5. Payroll
